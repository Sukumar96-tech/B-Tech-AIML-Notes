📖 About Us

Welcome to the AIML Notes Portal.

AIML Notes Portal is a platform developed to help students easily access academic resources such as notes, PDFs, Google Drive links, and study materials in one place.


Our mission is to make learning simple, organized, and accessible for every student by providing year-wise and subject-wise resources.


This project is developed by Sukumar, an Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning student passionate about Python, Flask, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Full Stack Development.
